Turn it on

  1. Click Open Safari Settings… above, or choose Safari, then Settings, then Extensions.
  2. Tick Clean Links.
  3. Choose Always Allow on Every Website so it can clean links everywhere.

You're all set

Links you visit, copy and share are cleaned automatically. Click the Clean Links button in Safari's toolbar to pause it, or to turn it off for a single site.

Free and open source. No accounts, no analytics, and nothing leaves your device.
