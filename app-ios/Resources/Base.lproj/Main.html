Clean Links

Share the link, not the tracking.

Turn it on in Safari

  1. Open the Settings app, then Apps, then Safari, then Extensions.
  2. Tap Clean Links and switch it on.
  3. Set All Websites to Allow, so it can clean links everywhere.

You can also do this from Safari: tap the aA button in the address bar, then Manage Extensions.

Using it

Links you open, copy and share are cleaned automatically. To pause it, or turn it off for one site, tap aA in Safari's address bar, then Clean Links.

Free and open source. No accounts, no analytics, and nothing leaves your device.
